Open Deep Research 💥

A team of contributors @ Hugging Face has unveiled Open Deep Research—an open alternative to OpenAI’s deep research tool.

While OpenAI’s version (currently in limited preview for ChatGPT Pro users at $200/month) compiles research reports by autonomously crawling the web, Hugging Face’s Open Deep Research takes it a step further with transparency and accessibility.

Powered by OpenAI’s proprietary o1 model and an open-source agentic framework for planning and tool integration.

Capable of autonomous web navigation, file manipulation, and running data calculations using a text-based browser and “text inspector” toolkit.

**Performance:** Achieved a 54% score on the GAIA benchmark for general AI assistants, compared to OpenAI’s 67.36%.

The source code is open on GitHub for the community to inspect and contribute.
