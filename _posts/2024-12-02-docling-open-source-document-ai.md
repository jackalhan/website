📄✨ Docling—an open-source project that bridges the gap between documents and generative AI! 🚀🤖

With Docling, you can effortlessly prepare files in various formats like PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and more, converting them into Markdown or JSON for seamless AI integration. 🛠️💡

Here’s some properties:
⚡ **Easy-to-use CLI** for quick and efficient workflows  
🔗 **Integration with top AI frameworks** like LlamaIndex and LangChain  
🔍 **Future-ready** with features like equation and metadata extraction on the horizon

Open source and supported by IBM
