Google DeepMind—introducing Genie 2, a revolutionary foundation world model that brings diverse, interactive 3D environments to life from just a single image prompt

- **Playable worlds** for humans and AI agents, perfect for training embodied AI.
- **Features realistic physics, object interactions, and long-horizon memory**, making it ideal for rapid prototyping and agent evaluation.
- **Builds on cutting-edge tech** like Imagen 3 and SIMA, pushing us closer to general AI.
- Powered by a **diffusion model architecture**, Genie 2 creates vast and varied training environments to unlock the potential of future AI systems.

This might address the challenge of access to rich, scalable training data for embodied agents. 🌟🚀
