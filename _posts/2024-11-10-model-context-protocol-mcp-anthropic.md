Anthropic has open-sourced the Model Context Protocol (MCP), a standardized interface enabling AI assistants to securely access diverse data sources. MCP streamlines integration, replacing custom connections with a single protocol for improved efficiency and scalability.

The initiative includes SDKs, pre-built servers for common platforms (like Google Drive and Slack), and an open-source repository. Early adopters are already leveraging MCP to enhance their AI tools, demonstrating its potential to improve context awareness and functionality. This open-source approach fosters collaboration and accelerates the development of context-aware AI. The goal is to create a more connected and sustainable AI ecosystem.
